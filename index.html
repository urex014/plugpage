FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can have a PlugPage. We do the writing, design and setup, and you just approve it.

Will this replace my WhatsApp business?

No, it powers it. Every “Order” button opens a chat with you on WhatsApp, so you keep selling the way you already do, just to more people.

How fast will my page be ready?

Usually within 24 hours of receiving your photos, prices and details. Pro updates are done within 12 hours.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or a secure payment link. You get a receipt every time. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time, and your page simply goes offline at the end of the paid month.

What does “PlugPage Verified” mean?

We confirm your phone number, your social media history, and your ID or CAC registration if you have one. It tells buyers a real person runs this business. It is not a guarantee of individual orders.

Can I use my own domain like myshop.com.ng?

Yes, on Pro. We help you buy and connect it, and you pay only what the domain registrar charges.

What if I don't like my page?

We revise it until you're happy before it goes live. If we can't get it right, we refund your setup fee.

How does the referral offer work?

Refer another seller and get 1 month free for each one who pays, with no limit. The seller you refer must pay for a PlugPage plan, and your free month is applied after their page goes live. When they message us, they should tell us your shop name (for example “Hi PlugPage, [your shop name] referred me”) so we can credit you.

Which businesses is PlugPage for?

Fashion, beauty, food, caterers, tailors, makeup artists, phone and gadget sellers, cleaners, tutors and event vendors. Anyone who sells through WhatsApp or Instagram in Nigeria.
