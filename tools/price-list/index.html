Tips for a price list that sells

  • Lead with your bestsellers, and keep names short.
  • Show delivery fees separately ("Delivery fee depends on area").
  • Update it whenever supplier prices change; old prices cause arguments.

What size is the image?

Status/Story size is 1080×1920 pixels (up to 15 items). Square is 1080×1080 pixels (up to 10 items), good for Instagram and Facebook posts.

Can I use it for services, not just products?

Yes. Hair, nails, makeup, tailoring, cleaning, photography: any list of services and prices works.

Is my information saved?

No. The image is drawn in your browser and downloaded straight to your phone. Nothing is sent to PlugPage.
